# Expanded library and movie-night layer

510 recipes total: the prior 110 plus 400 new dishes. The additions combine 20 specified pulse-and-vegetable fillings with 20 different preparation methods. These are structured original recipe variants, not 400 individually kitchen-tested recipes. Cooked ingredients must be ready-cooked where labeled; timings assume that starting point. All recipes are vegetarian with a plant-based finishing adaptation and remain subject to checking packaged ingredients. Category photos are representative.

100 inspired pairings span 20 movies and refer to 100 distinct existing recipe IDs. Renaming a recipe never increases the recipe count. 300 of the new recipes are outside the themed layer. Movie choices resolve by title and year through TMDB at selection time. A themed label is stored with a night only when recipe, movie title, and release year match. No official association or recipe-from-film claim is made.

Browsing supports category, cuisine inspiration, ingredient/name, time and 24-item paging. Movie-night browsing supports film/dish search and 20-item paging.
