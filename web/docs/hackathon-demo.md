# Crave Frame: one evening, fewer decisions

## The pitch
“Crave Frame turns two nightly decisions—what to eat and what to watch—into one coordinated evening.”

## A 90-second demo

| Time | Show | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–10s | Brief arrival / dinner / couch introduction; use Start my evening to skip if needed. | “It’s the end of a long day. We’re hungry, and we haven’t picked anything to watch.” |
| 10–25s | Search for a familiar title. Pick its exact movie or episode and let the real recommendations load. | “Choose the story first. Crave Frame suggests real recipes within our food and time preferences.” |
| 25–38s | Open Why this pairing on one returned meal. Point to the actual story cue and dinner explanation. | “Here’s the connection—and whether it’s documented food, setting inspiration, or simply a practical dinner.” |
| 38–50s | Review the recipe and ingredients, then Save this evening. | “We approve dinner before it becomes our plan.” |
| 50–65s | Open I’m delayed, select 15 minutes, preview, then apply. Show the new dinner and watch times. | “Real life intervenes. The schedule adapts while our chosen dinner and story stay together.” |
| 65–80s | Show cooking QR, open it on a phone, advance one recipe step and show the TV update. | “The TV keeps the evening visible. The phone takes the recipe into the kitchen.” |
| 80–90s | Open a previously completed demonstration evening; show reflection and Loved evenings. | “Afterward, we remember what worked—so the next evening starts with us.” |

## Choose an honest example
Use a well-known title you can identify exactly, such as Ratatouille. Select a pairing from the actual response; do not script an invented dish or a scene that the app cannot substantiate. The explanation’s strength matters more than a particular recipe.

## Alternate recovery beat
Replace the delay segment with Something changed? Adjust this evening. Select a missing ingredient and preview replacement dinners, or choose We changed our minds → Keep the story · different dinner. Show the preview and ingredient approval before applying. Keep this as an alternative, not an extra tour in the main demo.

## Operator preparation
- Open the current live TV page in Silk and keep a phone camera ready.
- Use a demonstration household with ordinary preferences and an unfinished plan. Prepare one completed favorite for the closing segment; disclose that it is an earlier example.
- Check the title identity, real AI response, QR readability, and phone-to-TV update beforehand. AI timing varies; a recorded walkthrough can include a clearly labeled time cut while it loads.
- Do not claim that Crave Frame streams movies, places delivery orders itself, or has verified on-screen food evidence unless the shown result supports it.
- Keep the private cooking link within the demonstration group. Disconnect it after a recorded demo.
- This script is drafted, not recorded or rehearsed. Confirm the event’s official video length and submission requirements before recording.

## Finish with one sentence
“Choose together, adapt when life changes, and make the evening worth repeating.”
