# Supper & Scene: live AI demonstration

Target 2 minutes 45 seconds. Primary submission direction: Alexa+ simulated experience, using Gemini on Vertex AI. This is an independent simulation, not a connected Alexa skill. The official rules permit an AI-powered web simulation without an MCP endpoint.

1. 0:00-0:20: Open Concierge. Explain the two starting points: dinner or a watch. Request two people, chicken tacos, an adventurous movie, 30 minutes to cook and three hours to watch.
2. 0:20-0:55: Compare choices, reasons and tradeoffs. Refine the request. Show time and food limits. Ambiguous movie editions and TV episodes require selection.
3. 0:55-1:25: Choose an evening, review ingredients and the supported adaptation, then confirm it. The AI explanation remains with the saved night. Suggestions alone do not change a saved plan.
4. 1:25-1:55: Before cooking, ask Rescue: We are 5 minutes late, keep the movie. Show the parsed change and alternatives; review before applying. Alternatively, upload a pantry photo and approve identified ingredients. Do not claim photo recognition verifies freshness or allergies.
5. 1:55-2:25: Scan the cooking QR on a phone. Ask the coach about a step. Advance a step and show the TV plan syncing. Do not claim real cooking was completed during the recording.
6. 2:25-2:45: Save explicit feedback, show changed recommendations, and open My taste. Distinguish saved preferences from AI interpretation.
